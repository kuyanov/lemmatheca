Keep the arithmetic axioms fixed while changing the logic

Use the full native arithmetic language with terms \(0,S,+,\cdot\), logical equality, and separate constructors for every connective and quantifier. Let \(H\) be the exact HA axiom set: the seven basic closed sentences and every full native induction axiom. Write \(H_X\) for its closed axioms renamed into the original free-name set \(X\).

An arithmetic certificate has a specified finite support in \(H_X\), together with an actual public deduction. We write \(\mathrm{HA},\Gamma\vdash_X A\) for existence of an intuitionistic certificate allowing the additional finite context \(\Gamma\). The notation \(\mathrm{HA}^{\mathrm C},\Gamma\vdash_X A\) uses the same arithmetic axioms and additionally permits classical contradiction. Logical equality is enabled in both systems. For a sentence and empty additional context, omit the subscripts and context.

Apply the existing negative translation to arithmetic formulas

Use the specified Gödel–Gentzen translation \(A^{\mathsf N}\). Arithmetic has no nonlogical relation symbols, so its atoms are equations, translated as \((s=t)^{\mathsf N}=\neg\neg(s=t)\). Falsum is fixed; negation, conjunction, implication and universal quantification preserve their outer constructors. Disjunction and existence satisfy

\[(B\lor C)^{\mathsf N}=\neg\neg(B^{\mathsf N}\lor C^{\mathsf N}),\qquad (\exists xB)^{\mathsf N}=\neg\neg\exists xB^{\mathsf N}.\]

Terms and available bound slots are unchanged. Put \(\Gamma^{\mathsf N}=\{B^{\mathsf N}:B\in\Gamma\}\). The logical proof transformation already interprets a classical tree from translated premises. To obtain an interpretation using the original arithmetic axioms \(H\), we must construct HA certificates of their translated versions.

Translate substitutions and finite universal closures literally

Negative translation commutes with the actual zero substitution, successor substitution, protected opening and finite universal closure used in arithmetic. In particular, for a full formula \(B(\bar y)\),

\[(\forall\bar y\,B)^{\mathsf N}=\forall\bar y\,B^{\mathsf N}.\]

The scope identities commute translation with every specified term map. Apply them to the maps that replace the distinguished induction variable by \(0\) or \(Sx\), fixing the parameter tuple. Translation retains each universal constructor, so induction on the length of the ordered parameter list proves the closure identity. Empty parameter lists are included. These are equalities of formula trees, including occurrences beneath nested binders.

A forbidden successor equation remains forbidden after translation

HA proves \(\forall x\,\neg\neg\neg(Sx=0)\), the translation of its successor-separation axiom.

At a protected arbitrary \(x\), the original axiom supplies \(\neg(Sx=0)\). Assume \(\neg\neg(Sx=0)\). This contradicts the supplied negative equation. Discharge the temporary double-negated equation to get \(\neg\neg\neg(Sx=0)\), then universally introduce \(x\). No elimination of double negation at an arbitrary formula was used.

Lift injectivity through double-negated equations

HA proves the literal translated injectivity sentence

\[\forall x\forall y\,\bigl(\neg\neg(Sx=Sy)\to\neg\neg(x=y)\bigr).\]

Fix protected \(x,y\) and assume \(\neg\neg(Sx=Sy)\). Under a further \(\neg(x=y)\), temporarily assume \(Sx=Sy\). Original successor injectivity gives \(x=y\), a contradiction. Discharge the successor equation to obtain \(\neg(Sx=Sy)\); this contradicts the first assumption. Discharge \(\neg(x=y)\), obtaining \(\neg\neg(x=y)\). Discharge the first assumption and close both protected parameters. Every step belongs to intuitionistic arithmetic.

Translate the predecessor witness inside its double negation

HA proves the translated predecessor axiom

\[\forall x\,\bigl(\neg\neg\neg(x=0)\to\neg\neg\exists y\,\neg\neg(x=Sy)\bigr).\]

Fix protected \(x\) and assume \(\neg\neg\neg(x=0)\). An assumed \(x=0\) would give \(\neg\neg(x=0)\) by double-negation introduction, and hence a contradiction. Discharge it to obtain \(\neg(x=0)\). The original predecessor axiom now gives an actual \(\exists y\,x=Sy\).

Eliminate that existential with protected witness \(e\). From the branch equation \(x=Se\), introduce \(\neg\neg(x=Se)\), then introduce \(\exists y\,\neg\neg(x=Sy)\) at that same \(e\), and double-negate the existential. This final formula contains no \(e\), so witness elimination concludes it. Discharge the initial assumption and universally introduce \(x\). The constructed certificate retains the original positive witness branch while producing precisely the recursively translated endpoint.

The translation of induction is induction for the translated body

For every full body \(\varphi(x,\bar y)\), write \(\operatorname{Ind}_{\bar y}(\varphi)\) for its exact closed native induction axiom. Then

\[\operatorname{Ind}_{\bar y}(\varphi)^{\mathsf N}=\operatorname{Ind}_{\bar y}(\varphi^{\mathsf N}).\]

Its base translates to \(\varphi^{\mathsf N}(0,\bar y)\), and its successor instance translates to \(\varphi^{\mathsf N}(Sx,\bar y)\), by . The conjunction of base and step, both implications and every universal constructor retain their constructors. These are exactly the components defining the induction axiom for \(\varphi^{\mathsf N}\), with the identical finite parameter list. Thus the displayed identity is literal, and its right side is itself an HA axiom. The original body may contain native disjunctions and existentials; the full induction schema permits its translated body too.

An existential induction body stays within the full schema

Take \(\varphi(x,y)\equiv\exists z\,(x+z=y)\), with induction variable \(x\) and fixed parameter \(y\). Its translation is exactly

\[\varphi^{\mathsf N}(x,y)=\neg\neg\exists z\,\neg\neg(x+z=y).\]

Accordingly, the translation of \(\operatorname{Ind}_y(\varphi)\) is the native induction axiom for this displayed translated body, still universally closed over \(y\). The full schema includes this translated instance even though its original body contains a native existential.

Specify a fragment before expanding its connectives

The disjunction- and existential-free native fragment consists of formulas built by

\[F::=\bot\mid s=t\mid\neg F\mid(F\land F)\mid(F\to F)\mid\forall xF.\]

The restriction applies to the whole native tree, including inside negations and quantified bodies. It is preserved by scope maps, since term replacement changes no formula constructor. Opening a body therefore remains in this fragment. Arbitrarily nested universal quantifiers and implications are allowed.

Arithmetic fixes this fragment up to constructive proof conversions

For every formula \(F\) in the specified fragment, supplied HA certificates of either \(F\) or \(F^{\mathsf N}\) convert into certificates of the other, in the same finite additional context and original free names.

Induct on original formula depth, keeping names and ambient contexts arbitrary. At an equation, the forward conversion introduces double negation; the backward conversion applies HA's proved atomic stability at the actual two terms. Falsum is unchanged. There is no relation-atom case in the arithmetic language.

For \(\neg B\), assume \(B^{\mathsf N}\), convert it back to \(B\), and contradict the given \(\neg B\); discharge to obtain \(\neg B^{\mathsf N}\). Reverse the child conversion for the converse. For conjunction, project each factor, apply its child conversion and conjoin. For \(B\to C\), assume \(B^{\mathsf N}\), convert it backwards to \(B\), apply the given implication, convert \(C\) forwards and discharge. In the reverse direction use the child conversions in reverse order.

For a universal, lift the given certificate to a protected arbitrary parameter and instantiate it there. Apply the smaller opened-body conversion in that lifted context, then universally introduce again. The opening identity identifies the translated endpoints. Closing the protected branch preserves finite arithmetic supports as in HA's quantifier rules. No disjunction or existential case remains in the specified fragment.

The fragment test concerns the original native tree. A smaller classical existential is an abbreviation: for an equation body \(E(x)\), its code is \((\forall x(E(x)\to\bot))\to\bot\). Its literal native inclusion lies in the fragment, so a PA proof of the small code yields an HA proof of its native inclusion. The classical presentation bridge supplies conversions between such readings in classical deduction. It supplies no intuitionistic operation turning this code proof into a native witness proof. The conservation statement therefore retains the native conclusion's explicit syntactic restriction; expanding away its positive constructors cannot justify removing it.

The assertion proved here is external: a supplied contradiction certificate in either calculus yields one in the other.
