Independently review the human text of a Lemmatheca entry before formalization.

Input
- Entry ID: <entry-id>

Read README.md, corpus/README.md, docs/entry-sources.md, and the complete entry,
including its metadata, cited source passages, linked prerequisites, figures,
proofs, and hidden question answers. Judge the current text on its merits rather
than relying on a previous AI's description of its work.

Check the following:
- Every mathematical assertion has the right hypotheses, quantifiers, domain,
  codomain, and conclusion. Test empty, singleton, boundary, finite, and infinite
  cases where relevant. Check both directions of equivalences and verify concrete
  examples and counterexamples, including the labels and arrows in figures.
- Proofs establish what is claimed, do not assume their conclusions, and explain
  the important steps at the intended reader's level. Questions have correct
  answers; illustrative analogies do not substitute for mathematical statements.
- Definitions and notation are consistent, and prerequisites are introduced or
  linked before use. Citations support the material and are accurately recorded.
- The prose has no redundant words, filler, repeated definitions, repeated proof
  conclusions, or unnecessary meta-commentary. Prefer direct statements and short
  explanations. Preserve necessary hypotheses, examples, and logical transitions;
  concision must not create a mathematical gap.
- HTML, metadata, references, reading order, equations, and assets follow the
  current repository contract. Inspect the rendered page if available. Missing
  formal mappings are expected at this stage and do not make the prose defective.

Run uv run python app/manage.py validate_corpus. Report findings in order of
importance, with file/line or block references, the reason each issue matters,
and a concrete suggested correction. Distinguish mathematical errors from optional
editorial improvements. If no issue is found, say what you checked and what remains
unverified. End with a concise list of changes needed before formalization.

This is a review: do not rewrite the entry, add formal nodes, record approval, or
publish it. A maintainer can request the proposed revisions in a follow-up.
