If \(A,B,C\) are nonempty, then both \(A+B\) and \((A+B)+C\) are nonempty.

Proof

The {% math_ref "nonempty-sumset" entry_id="thm-sumset-lower-bound" from_block="nonempty-triple-sumset" %} lemma gives an element of \(A+B\). Applying it again to \(A+B\) and \(C\) gives an element of \((A+B)+C\). Both sets are finite because each sum is formed from finitely many choices. □
