Must adding a third nonempty set strictly increase the size of \(A+B\)?

Show answerHide answer

No. For \(A=B=C=\{0\}\) in the integers,

\[ A+B=(A+B)+C=\{0\}. \]

More generally, {% math_ref "zero-summand" entry_id="thm-sumset-lower-bound" from_block="strict-growth-question" %} shows that choosing \(C=\{0\}\) leaves any sumset \(A+B\) unchanged.
