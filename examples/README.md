# Draft corpus examples

These JSON and Markdown files make the proposal concrete. They are **unpublished,
AI-authored design examples**. No maintainer has accepted them and no full Lean
mathematical dependency audit has run.

The [Lean project](../formal/README.md) checks both the general sumset lower bound
(a lemma in the first note) and a triple-sumset bound (a theorem in the second note) that applies it twice. It also
retains the concrete integer illustration `{0, 1} + {0, 2} = {0, 1, 2, 3}` and two
supporting translation lemmas. Successful builds and axiom checks are recorded in
`formal/checks/sumsets.json`; they do not grant editorial acceptance.

`taxonomy.json` demonstrates areas, subareas, and topics. Its small initial tree
is extensible and does not claim complete coverage. `external-lemmas.json` is an
empty draft registry; it grants no exceptions.

`sumsets/entries.json` defines two paper-like reading entries with ordered blocks:
**Sumsets and translations** and **Adding three sets**. Each block has a stable ID,
kind, title, trusted HTML template, and optional formal binding or linked record.
References identify both the target article and the target block, with the article
revision. Local display numbers are generated by kind, starting at 1 in each entry.
Question templates contain a collapsed native HTML answer. The extra nonemptiness,
neutral-summand, and counterexample results have Lean bindings directly on their
blocks; the existing main results reuse the separate mathematical records below.

`sumsets/definition.json` describes a definition.
`sumsets/lower-bound.json` describes the lemma (its original stable `thm-` ID is retained).
`sumsets/proofs/translation.json` describes one proof, pinned to the statement's
draft revision. `sumsets/triple-lower-bound.json` and
`sumsets/proofs/triple-lower-bound.json` describe the second statement and proof,
with explicit references to the lemma's revision. `text` and `verification_report`
paths are relative to their own JSON files; `source` paths are repository-relative. Related
entry IDs and prerequisite proposals are explicit.

Prerequisites marked `planned` deliberately do not resolve to accepted entries.
The future publication validator must reject these records until the prerequisites
are resolved, verification and dependency checks pass, and a human maintainer approves.
These files are examples of a proposed format, not its production JSON Schema.
