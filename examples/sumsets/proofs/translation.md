# Translate each summand into the sumset

Choose \(b_0\in B\). For every \(a\in A\), the element \(a+b_0\)
belongs to \(A+B\). Moreover,

\[
a+b_0=a'+b_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=a',
\]

by cancellation. Thus \(a\mapsto a+b_0\) is an injection from \(A\)
into \(A+B\), giving \(|A|\leq |A+B|\).

Choose \(a_0\in A\) and apply the same argument to \(b\mapsto a_0+b\).
This gives \(|B|\leq |A+B|\). Since \(|A+B|\) is at least each of the two
cardinalities, it is at least their maximum.
